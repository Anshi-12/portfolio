📚 From my bookshelf

Research that treats missing data as a clue.

"In the ICU, what doesn't get measured, and when, says a lot about the patient."

  • The question. Can a Transformer flag sepsis early from irregular, gap-filled ICU vitals in the PhysioNet/CinC 2019 dataset?
  • The idea. A missingness-aware encoder with a tripartite embedding of values, missingness mask and time since last measurement, compared with a vanilla Transformer.
  • The context. Deep Learning Research Intern, IEEE Computer Society Bangalore, IAMPro 2026. I'm currently writing the paper.
